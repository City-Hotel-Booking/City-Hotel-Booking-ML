# Data inspection and EDA

Status: template. Owner: Member 1.

## Source verification

Record source, checksum, dimensions, exact labels, data types, category counts, and date ranges.

## Findings and evidence

Summarize missing values, padded text, not-applicable values, repeated rows, target distributions, unusual guest/stay counts, skew, and ADR outliers. Link actual tables/figures under outputs/data and outputs/eda; explain their meaning.

## Proposed treatments

For each proposal, record the reason, affected rows, and whether it is approved or pending in decisions.md. Do not alter the raw file or automatically delete all identical records.

## Handoff

Give Member 2 the source checks and treatment evidence. Give Members 3-5 task-relevant findings and Member 6 checked figure paths and a reproduction command.
